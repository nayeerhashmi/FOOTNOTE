Issue 001 — The Sound We Made

Before the beat,
there was rhythm.

The first issue looks at how sound and music shape cultural identity. Contemporary hip-hop sits at its centre, alongside the folk traditions, regional languages and histories that continue to influence how people create.

It is a work in progress. Interviews, research, image permissions and fact-checking come before publication.

04 / About the publication

A footnote
is never
just a footnote.

FOOTNOTE is an independent magazine and cultural archive documenting the voices, music, languages, creative scenes and stories of Jammu and Kashmir. Hip-hop is central to its contemporary cultural coverage, but the publication is wider than any one genre.

We work across reporting, interviews, essays, criticism, photography, archival research and oral history. We value primary sources, clear attribution, cultural context and the rights of the people whose work we document.

This site is the beginning of that archive. It will grow issue by issue, story by story.

AccuracyNames, dates and claims are checked before publication.
AttributionSources, contributors and archives are credited.
RespectPeople and cultures are represented with context.
